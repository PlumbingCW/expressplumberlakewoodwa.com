Where we serve in Fircrest, and the one ZIP you share

Fircrest has a single ZIP, 98466, and it also serves part of University Place. Give your street address, not only the ZIP, so the dispatcher knows which city you are in. We also reach DuPont on the same line.

Split-levels and ramblers: what 62 percent of the city's homes mean for drains

The 2024 city plan says 70 percent of Fircrest housing is single-family detached and that 62 percent of the stock is split-level and rambler homes from the 1950s through the 1970s. Older Census figures give a median build year of 1968, with about 37 percent built before 1960 and about 38 percent between 1960 and 1979.

In a split-level, the lowest bathroom or laundry sits closest to the sewer line. That is where a backup shows first, often as a gurgle in a floor drain or a toilet that rises when the washer drains. If that happens, stop using water and call.

A camera look through the cleanout tells us whether it is roots, grease or a belly in the pipe. See sewer camera inspection for how that works, and drain cleaning for the clearing step.

Permits at the Planning and Building counter

Plumbing permits, water heaters included, go through the city's Planning and Building permit center. A personal sewer-line repair has a no-fee Public Works permit, needed only if the work cuts into the street or right-of-way.

Our water heater replacement visits start with the tank's age and strapping. State code requires heaters to be strapped, and we tell you if the job looks like it needs a permit.
