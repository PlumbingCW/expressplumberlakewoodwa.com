Where Tillicum sits

The city's neighborhood plan describes Tillicum as a planning area of roughly 330 acres. Interstate 5 and the railroad corridor form its southeast edge. Camp Murray lies to the southwest, the American Lake shoreline to the northwest and private gated communities to the northeast.

The city's neighborhood association is called Tillicum/Woodbrook, and the community center is at 14916 Washington Ave SW. The city's neighborhood plan says Tillicum's housing sheltered soldiers in both world wars. That background is useful context, but a build date is not a diagnosis, because it does not tell a plumber what is inside your walls.

From septic to sewer: what it means at your house

Tillicum and Woodbrook relied on septic systems until county sewer service arrived, in 2011 for Tillicum and 2012 for Woodbrook, according to a 2020 city FAQ. The city built the sewer and then turned it over to Pierce County to operate. That change makes one question worth asking first: is your property connected to sewer, or still on septic?

Until you know, avoid chemical drain openers on a slow drain. They can harm a septic system and are hard on pipes. Do not keep running a fixture that is backing up, and shut off the main water valve if a pipe is leaking.

  1. 1

    Find out your status

    Ask Pierce County Public Works and Utilities, which handles sewer connections in Lakewood, or check your utility bill.

  2. 2

    Know the clock

    Lakewood's municipal code lets a working septic system stay up to 10 years after notice that sewer is available. Health department certification is needed beyond 5 years.

  3. 3

    Know the triggers

    Connection becomes mandatory on septic failure, heavier use, new construction occupancy, a sale, a subdivision or a nuisance order.

  4. 4

    Plan the tank

    Decommissioning an old tank follows Tacoma-Pierce County Health Department rules, and tank work is a separate trade from plumbing.

Renting in Tillicum? Start with your landlord

Rental housing has long been a large part of Tillicum, according to the city's neighborhood plan, though the figures there are old. If you rent, tell the owner or manager about the problem before you book a plumber. Many leases require the owner to authorize repairs.

Know where your main water shut-off is and keep the manager's phone number handy. Then a plumber can talk to the right person on the first call.
